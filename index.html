BoneShakers Social Club

The BoneShakers Social Club is a community-based nonprofit motorcycle social club based in Virginia Beach, Virginia, serving the Hampton Roads community through charity rides, fundraising events, and our annual Bikes for Tykes program..

A legacy of brotherhood and sisterhood, rides, and giving back.
