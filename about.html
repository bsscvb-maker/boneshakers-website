About BoneShakers

A Virginia Beach riding family — not an MC, not a brand — built on respect, service, and showing up when it matters.

Who We Are

BoneShakers Social Club is a community of riders connected by friendship, accountability, and a shared commitment to community involvement.

We Ride Together
Organized rides, signature events, and a focus on safety, respect, and enjoyment.
We Serve Together
Charity rides, fundraisers, parades, and hands-on community support.
We Stand Together
Support for our members, their families, and those in need — on and off the road.

Our Story

Founded in Virginia Beach in 2005, BoneShakers began as a small group of riders who believed motorcycling should center on camaraderie, responsibility, and community.

The club grew steadily — not by chasing numbers or recognition — but by staying grounded in the values that brought us together.

We are not an MC. We do not claim territory or hierarchy. We are a social riding family focused on enjoying the road and supporting one another.

Today, BoneShakers is proud to be part of the local fabric through rides, fundraisers, memorials, parades, and community partnerships.

Our legacy is measured in relationships built, causes supported, and moments shared — not titles or status.

Our commitment is simple: enjoy the ride, serve with intention, and be there when it counts.

What We Believe

These principles guide how we ride, how we serve, and how we treat one another.

Respect — for the road, the club, and the community.
Accountability — to each other and to the causes we support.
Consistency — showing up isn’t optional; it’s who we are.

Recognition & Appreciation

The BoneShakers Social Club does not seek recognition — our focus is on service, brotherhood, and community support.

From time to time, our efforts are acknowledged by the organizations and communities we stand alongside.

These certificates represent partnerships built on trust, action, and follow-through.

Certificate of Appreciation
Hells Angels Beach Side — Annual Food Drive
January 11, 2025

This recognition reflects a shared commitment to community outreach and direct action. Members of the BoneShakers Social Club worked alongside Hells Angels Beach Side to support local families through coordinated effort, accountability, and service where it mattered most.

Certificate of Appreciation
Mutts With A Mission — Blue Level Sponsor
August 5, 2025

As a Blue Level Sponsor, the BoneShakers Social Club proudly supported Mutts With A Mission in their mission to train service dogs for veterans and first responders. This partnership reflects a shared belief in honoring service, loyalty, and sacrifice through action that truly matters.

Why This Recognition Matters

This recognition exists because of Athena.

Athena was more than a companion—she was family. Her loyalty, quiet strength, and unconditional love left a lasting imprint on those who knew her. Like so many dogs who walk beside us through life, she represented comfort, presence, and an unbreakable bond.

When Athena was lost, her memory became a reason to give back. What began as remembrance turned into purpose—supporting Mutts With A Mission and their work rescuing dogs and training them to serve veterans and first responders. This recognition stands as a reminder that one dog’s life can inspire meaningful, lasting action.
