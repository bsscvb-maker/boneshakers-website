Become a Community Partner

Sponsorships directly support local community initiatives, including our Bikes for Tykes program and real-world assistance for families, veterans, and neighbors who need help.

Our Impact

We don’t just fund “big causes.” We help real people — right here at home. From our Bikes for Tykes mission to local assistance that keeps families moving forward, Bone Shakers Social Club puts sponsorship dollars to work in the community.

  • 67 bicycles purchased last year for local children
  • $28,000+ returned to the community in 2025
  • $40,000+ projected community impact in 2026
  • Food support, memorial rides, and direct local assistance to neighbors in need

Trusted by Local Businesses

Proudly supported by trusted contractors, skilled trades, equipment services, and community-focused local businesses across Hampton Roads.

What Happens After You Sponsor

  1. Select a sponsorship level below (or request an invoice).
  2. Your business is confirmed and recorded as an official Community Partner.
  3. BSSC leadership coordinates branding details (logo, link, and recognition schedule).
  4. You’re recognized at meetings and applicable events based on tier benefits.

We run sponsorships like a real organization: clear benefits, clean communication, and consistent recognition throughout the year.
