Support the Mission

Partner with BoneShakers Social Club and help us continue supporting children, families, veterans, local organizations, and community causes throughout Hampton Roads.

Become a Sponsor

BoneShakers works with businesses, organizations, and individuals who believe in giving back locally. Sponsorship helps support our community programs, charity events, rides, and Bikes for Tykes.

Whether you want to support a specific program, sponsor an event, contribute an auction or raffle item, or make a general contribution, we appreciate every partner who helps us keep the mission moving.

Need Documentation?

If your business or organization needs donation or sponsorship documentation, we can provide supporting information for your records.
