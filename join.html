Why Become a Member of the BoneShakers Social Club?

When you join, you’re not just signing up for a ride — you’re becoming part of a family. For over 20 years, the BoneShakers have been a respected riding organization dedicated to fun, unity, and giving back.

What Membership Means

A strong riding family, meaningful events, and a real opportunity to make an impact right here in our community.

Brotherhood & Sisterhood

A riding family that’s got your back — on and off the road.

Events & Rides

Parades, rallies, charity runs, and official club rides you can count on.

Exclusive Perks

BBQs, bike nights, family events, plus member-only discounts and early access.

Community Impact

Support kids, veterans, and local causes through organized charity efforts.

Your Voice Counts

Vote on club matters, policies, and help shape the next 20 years.

Tradition & Respect

Your Path to Membership

We take the time to get to know one another so every new member understands and shares our values.

Phase 1

Initial Interest & Inquiry

Show up, say hello, learn about us.

Attend open events, get referred by a member (encouraged, not required), and share basic information and why you want to join.

Phase 2

Meet & Greet / Hang-Around

About 3 club events or meetings.

Join at least three rides, events, or volunteer activities. No colors or patches yet — just get to know the crew.

Phase 3

Basic Background Screening

Safety and reputation check.

Valid license with M-endorsement and insurance; no disqualifying crimes. The Board may verify when concerns arise.

Phase 4

Prospect Phase

Minimum 3 months.

You’ll have a sponsor, pitch in at events, log service hours, and demonstrate reliability and safe conduct.

Phase 5

Vote for Full Membership

Board first, then membership.

Your sponsor presents you. The Board confirms requirements and votes. If approved, members may vote at the next meeting.

Phase 6

Patching-In

Welcome aboard.

We hold a patch-in and celebrate. New members are formally welcomed into the club and receive their colors.
