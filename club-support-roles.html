2026 Club Support Roles - Who We Are

The ride support roles that help keep our members safe, organized, and connected.

Road Captain - Austin

As Road Captain, I help ensure every ride is safe, organized, and enjoyable for our members. I take pride in planning routes, coordinating ride logistics, and setting the pace so everyone knows where they should be and what to expect.

My role goes beyond leading rides—I support the club’s mission by protecting our members on the road and helping every ride reflect the values and professionalism of our club.

Tail Gunner

As Tail Gunner, I help ensure the safety and integrity of the ride from the rear of the group. I take pride in watching over the pack, monitoring spacing, and assisting riders who may experience issues along the way.

My role goes beyond riding at the back—I support the club’s mission by making sure no member is left behind and that every ride remains safe, controlled, and respectful on the road.
