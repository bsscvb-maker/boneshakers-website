BoneShakers Social Club — Riding with Purpose

The BoneShakers Social Club (BSSC) has been part of the Hampton Roads community for over 21 years. We are more than a group of motorcycle enthusiasts—we are a family united by our love for riding and our commitment to giving back. Through countless events, fundraisers, and partnerships, our club has proudly contributed over $432,000 to local families, charities, and causes. Every mile we ride strengthens our bond as a club and fuels our mission to make a positive impact.

Bikes for Tykes

One of our signature events is the Bikes for Tykes Giveaway, where we work year-round to provide children in need with brand-new bicycles. Each bike is more than just a gift—it’s a symbol of freedom, confidence, and joy. For over 10 years, this event has reflected the heart of who we are: riders dedicated to creating opportunities for kids and strengthening our community one smile at a time.

Code of Conduct & Violations

BSSC is a family-friendly riding club. We expect respectful behavior at all club functions, online spaces, and partner events. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from events or loss of membership, per our bylaws.

  • Respect members, guests, venues, and partners.
  • No harassment, threats, or illegal activity.
  • Follow ride captains’ safety directions and venue rules.
  • Report issues to an officer or via the contact form.
