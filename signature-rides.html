  • Classic poker-run fun with stops that showcase local favorites. We ride staggered, keep formations tight, and make it friendly for newer riders. Funds raised support causes close to home. Keep your best card face down and your visor up.

    There is a Fisher House located at every major military medical center to assist families in need and to ensure that they are provided with the comforts of home in a supportive environment. Annually, the Fisher House Program serves more than 11,000 families and has made available nearly 3 million days of lodging to family members since the program originated in 1990. There is no charge for families to stay at a Fisher House operated by the Department of Veterans Affairs. The Fisher House Foundation uses donations to support the individual Fisher Houses which are operated by the Navy, Army, and Air Force around the country.

  • The Bug Run is one of our club’s most unique and entertaining traditions. Riders head out into the backroads and swampy stretches of Virginia Beach’s Pungo area, known for its winding lanes, open fields, and bug-heavy air.

    Participants wear stickers on their vests or helmets as targets, and whoever collects the most bug splatters and marks along the route takes home the win. Bring a passenger and get a couple of extra patches—boosting your chances to rack up even more bug hits and increase the odds of winning.

    It’s part competition, part comedy, and all camaraderie—an event that mixes backwoods adventure with laughter as members compare their “battle scars” at the finish line. The Bug Run celebrates the fun side of riding while keeping alive the playful spirit that makes our club more than just a group of bikers—it’s family.

  • Ride in Virginia Beach is an annual memorial motorcycle ride that honors the 31 service members, including 17 Navy SEALs from the area, who died in a 2011 helicopter crash in Afghanistan. The event, hosted by groups like the Virginia Patriot Guard, involves a 31-mile ride with each mile representing one of the fallen heroes, often including a stop to read their names and support the 31Heroes Project, a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization that supports Gold Star families and veterans' mental health treatment.

  • Each year, our members and volunteers come together for the Bikes for Tykes Build, where teamwork and community spirit shine brightest. Side by side, we assemble brand-new bicycles—each one destined to bring joy to a child in need. Wrenches turn, tires snap into place, and every finished bike becomes more than just two wheels: it becomes a symbol of hope, freedom, and a brighter tomorrow. The Build is about more than mechanics—it’s about the bonds we create while working together to make a difference.
  • The Giveaway is the heart of our mission. After the hard work of building, we gather as a community to place these bikes into the hands of excited children and their families. Each bike represents opportunity, confidence, and a little more happiness in our neighborhoods. The Giveaway isn’t just the end of the project—it’s the beginning of new adventures for kids across our community.
